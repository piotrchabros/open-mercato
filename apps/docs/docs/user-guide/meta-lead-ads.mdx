# Set up Meta Lead Ads

The Meta Lead Ads integration delivers every Instant Form submission from your Facebook and Instagram ads into the CRM within seconds. For each lead it creates or updates a person (and a company when the form asks for one), adds a **Lead from Meta** activity with all answers and campaign details, and can optionally create a deal and notify the assigned sales rep.

## What it does

- Receives lead notifications from Meta through a webhook and fetches the full lead from the Graph API.
- Matches existing people in the same organization by email, then by phone, so repeated submissions do not create duplicates.
- Records the answers, campaign, ad set, ad, form and platform on the person's activity history.
- Retries failed imports automatically and lets you retry or backfill manually.
- Reconciles the last 48 hours against Meta every night to catch missed leads.

## Prerequisites on Meta's side

Do these once in [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com):

1. Create a **Business** type app and add **Facebook Login for Business**. Create a login configuration and note its **Configuration ID**.
2. Add the **Webhooks** product, choose the **Page** object and subscribe to the **`leadgen`** field (see [Webhook](#webhook)).
3. Request these permissions: `leads_retrieval`, `pages_manage_metadata`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement` and `ads_management`.
4. Complete **App Review** and Business Verification so the app works for accounts that are not app testers.
5. Make sure the connecting Facebook user has **Leads Access** for each Page: in Meta Business Suite open **Business settings → Integrations → Leads Access** and grant your CRM app access to the Page.

## Configure the app in Open Mercato

Set these environment variables:

| Variable | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `OM_META_LEAD_ADS_APP_ID` | Meta app ID |
| `OM_META_LEAD_ADS_APP_SECRET` | Meta app secret (also verifies webhook signatures) |
| `OM_META_LEAD_ADS_VERIFY_TOKEN` | Token Meta echoes when you subscribe the webhook |
| `OM_META_LEAD_ADS_LOGIN_CONFIG_ID` | Facebook Login for Business configuration ID |
| `OM_META_LEAD_ADS_GRAPH_VERSION` | Optional Graph API version (default `v24.0`) |

Then copy them into every organization's encrypted credentials:

```bash
yarn mercato meta_lead_ads configure-from-env
```

Use `--tenant <tenantId>` to limit the run to one tenant and `--force` to overwrite credentials that already exist. You can also enter the values under **Settings → Integrations → Meta Lead Ads**.

## Webhook

In the Meta app open **Webhooks → Page** and set:

- **Callback URL**: `https://<your-domain>/api/meta_lead_ads/webhook`
- **Verify token**: the value of `OM_META_LEAD_ADS_VERIFY_TOKEN`

Subscribe the app to the **`leadgen`** field. Every notification is verified with the app secret before it is accepted. For local development expose the app through an HTTPS tunnel (for example `ngrok http 3000`) and use that URL.

## Connect a Page

1. Open **Meta Lead Ads** in the backend (**Connections**).
2. Click **Connect Meta account** and log in with Facebook. Grant all requested permissions.
3. Under **Pages**, assign each Page you want to the current organization. A Page can belong to only one organization across the whole installation.
4. The Page is subscribed to lead notifications automatically and shows a status: active, missing permissions, token expired or no leads access.

Users need the **Manage Meta Lead Ads** permission to connect and configure, and **View Meta Lead Ads logs** to see the leads log.

## Forms, field mapping and deal rules

Open a Page's forms and select a form to configure it:

- **Enable** the form so its leads are imported.
- **Field mapping**: email, phone, name and company are mapped by default. Map any custom question to a person or company custom field. Consent checkboxes are stored on the person as separate attributes.
- **Create a deal**: turn it on and choose the pipeline and stage.
- **Owner**: none, a fixed user, or round-robin across several users. The owner receives an in-app notification.

If Meta changes a form's questions, the form is flagged as needing attention until you review the mapping. A failed deal step (for example a deleted pipeline stage) flags it too; the person and activity are still saved.

## Monitor leads

Open **Recent leads** to see each lead event with its status (received, processed, duplicate, skipped, retrying, failed, dead-lettered, unrouted), source and error. Select failed or dead-lettered rows and choose **Retry**.

You can send a **test lead** for a form to check the full path end to end.

## Backfill and reconciliation

- **Backfill**: from a form, choose a date range (at most **90 days**) to import leads that arrived while the integration was off. Imported leads are deduplicated, so re-running is safe.
- **Nightly reconciliation**: every night the last 48 hours of Meta leads are compared with imported ones and any gap is filled. Runs are listed under sync runs.
- A daily token check marks connections as expired or invalid and alerts administrators.

## Privacy

- Raw lead payloads are deleted after **30 days** by default. Change the retention (1–365 days) in **Meta Lead Ads → Settings**.
- Deleting a person in the CRM also deletes that person's lead events.
- Meta's **data deletion callback URL** is `https://<your-domain>/api/meta_lead_ads/data-deletion`. Enter it in the app's settings under **Data deletion request URL**; Meta users who remove your app then have their data removed and receive a status link.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| Page status **no_leads_access** | Grant the CRM app **Leads Access** for the Page in Meta Business Suite (Business settings → Integrations → Leads Access), then run **Check** on the Page. |
| Connection **token_expired** | The Facebook login expired or was revoked. Reconnect the Meta account; Pages stay assigned. |
| Lead **dead-lettered** | Five automatic attempts (after 1 min, 5 min, 15 min and 40 min) failed. Read the error code in the leads log, fix the cause (token, access, mapping), then retry the event. |
| No leads arrive | Check that the Page is active, the form is enabled, the `leadgen` field is subscribed and the callback URL and verify token match. |
| Status **unrouted** | The lead's Page is not assigned to any organization. Assign the Page. |
